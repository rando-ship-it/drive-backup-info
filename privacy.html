Privacy Policy

This policy describes Personal Drive Backup, a utility intended for its owner’s personal backup and recovery use.

Google Drive access

The utility requests the Google Drive drive.file permission. It accesses files created by this OAuth app or explicitly authorized for it, and their metadata, to create, inspect, manage, and restore personal backups.

Storage and security

Backup contents are encrypted locally by restic before upload to the owner’s Google Drive. OAuth credentials and backup encryption keys are stored on owner-controlled computers. When a backup is restored, decrypted files are written to the owner’s computer.

Use and sharing

Google user data is used only for the owner-directed backup and recovery operations described here. It is not sold or used for advertising, marketing, or AI model training. Google processes stored data under its own policies.

Control and deletion

The owner can revoke this app’s access through Google Account permissions and can delete backup files from Google Drive. Revoking access does not itself delete existing backups.

This information website

This website has no forms, sign-in, advertising, or analytics scripts. It does not publish backup data or credentials. The website hosting provider may retain standard connection logs under its own policies.

Contact

For app-related questions, use the support email shown on the app’s Google authorization screen.
