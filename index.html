Personal Drive Backup

A personal utility used by its owner to create and restore encrypted computer backups in their own Google Drive.

Access is authorized through Google. Locally installed rclone transfers authorized files, and restic encrypts backup contents before upload.

This utility is intended for its owner’s personal use. This website provides information only; it does not provide sign-in or accept file uploads.
